# Prologue

**Before the legend, there was a Prologue.**

A free Become a Legend companion for SP Football Life 2026 and 2027. You create your
player — background, family, ambitions — and Prologue reads your save and tells
the career around them.

**[Download the latest release](https://github.com/ahmadsk-cell/Prologue/releases/latest)** ·
[Install guide](https://askandalus.com/downloads/prologue/install-guide.html) ·
[askandalus.com/downloads/prologue](https://askandalus.com/downloads/prologue)

This repository holds Prologue's releases. Download the `Prologue-<version>.zip`
asset from a release, not the "Source code" archives GitHub adds automatically.

## What it does

- Messages from your manager, agent, teammates, family and coaches, and decisions
  waiting for you, plus a dressing-room chat after every match
- A living world: a social feed and a newspaper that react to every match, the
  press, sponsors whose deals move your following, and the national team
- AI clubs that buy, sell and loan players, with each transfer window's moves in
  World > Transfers
- Club and national rankings, the game's individual awards, worldwide top scorers
  and assist providers across every competition, and every honour your player wins
- Small attribute boosts from training, sponsors and honours, written only to your
  own player, never more than ±3 at a time and read back afterwards
- Momentum Shift, as in Touchline: each month every player's overall, yours included,
  rises or falls for the month ahead on how much he played and how well, and a season
  above himself keeps a point (Settings > Momentum Shift)
- Plays well with a controller, handhelds included, and BACK TO GAME puts Football Life
  in front
- Your overall worked out from your attributes in the running game, as Football Life
  works it out
- Reads your save and writes to it only for a loan, when you accept one and when you
  come home, after backing it up and with Football Life closed
- Regens with newblood: retired players come back with new names, faces and potential
- Works with FL Mod Studio, its FL27 Gigapack included (Settings > FL Mod Studio)
- Updates itself from this page, checked against the published checksum
- In English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian, German, Polish, Russian,
  Indonesian, Turkish, Vietnamese, Arabic, Thai, Japanese and Simplified Chinese
  (Settings > Language), stories and messages included
- Player faces from the game in squad rows, rival cards and messages, or a PNG
  of your own

## Before you install

- Football Life 2026 or 2027. Reading your OVR works on any version; attribute boosts
  are written straight away on FL26 version 1.4 and on FL27, and on other versions once
  your player's record in the game matches your save.
- Sider, with the game started through it
- A Become a Legend career, or one you are about to start
- Windows 10 or 11. Touchline is only needed for crowd attendance.

## Install

1. Download `Prologue-<version>.zip` from the
   [latest release](https://github.com/ahmadsk-cell/Prologue/releases/latest).
2. Extract it completely. Do not run anything from inside the zip.
3. Run `PrologueSetup.exe` and follow the
   [install guide](https://askandalus.com/downloads/prologue/install-guide.html).

Setup adds a PrologueCompanion folder with the Prologue program and creates
`content\prologue`, where your careers are kept. If your Sider folder has no
newblood (the regen module Touchline also uses), setup adds it and its lines to
`sider.ini`, backing `sider.ini` up first. Nothing else in `sider.ini` changes.
Setup never deletes anything and never touches a career you already have.

## If Windows warns you

Prologue is not code-signed, so Windows may say it "protected your PC." Choose
**More info**, then **Run anyway**, or first check the zip against the
`SHA256SUMS.txt` published with each release.
