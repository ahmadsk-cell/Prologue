# Prologue

**Before the legend, there was a Prologue.**

A free Become a Legend companion for SP Football Life 2026. You create your
player — background, family, ambitions — and Prologue reads your save and tells
the career around them.

**[Download the latest release](https://github.com/ahmadsk-cell/Prologue/releases/latest)** ·
[Install guide](https://askandalus.com/downloads/prologue/install-guide.html) ·
[askandalus.com/downloads/prologue](https://askandalus.com/downloads/prologue)

This repository holds Prologue's releases. Download the `Prologue-<version>.zip`
asset from a release, not the "Source code" archives GitHub adds automatically.

## What it does

- Messages from your manager, agent, teammates, family and coaches, and decisions
  waiting for you
- The press, sponsors and the national team, reacting to how your career goes
- Club and national rankings, the game's individual awards, and every honour your
  player wins
- Small attribute boosts from training, sponsors and honours, written only to your
  own player, never more than ±3 at a time and read back afterwards
- Reads your save and never edits it; Football Life still writes it itself
- In English, Spanish and Portuguese (Settings > Appearance > Language). Stories
  are in English for now.

## Before you install

- Football Life 2026. Attribute boosts and reading your OVR need version 1.4;
  other versions still get everything built from your save.
- Sider, with the game started through it
- A Become a Legend career, or one you are about to start
- Windows 10 or 11. Touchline is not needed.

## Install

1. Download `Prologue-<version>.zip` from the
   [latest release](https://github.com/ahmadsk-cell/Prologue/releases/latest).
2. Extract it completely. Do not run anything from inside the zip.
3. Run `PrologueSetup.exe` and follow the
   [install guide](https://askandalus.com/downloads/prologue/install-guide.html).

Setup adds a PrologueCompanion folder with the Prologue program and creates
`content\prologue`, where your careers are kept. It adds no Sider scripts, never
edits `sider.ini`, never deletes anything and never touches a career you already
have.

## If Windows warns you

Prologue is not code-signed, so Windows may say it "protected your PC." Choose
**More info**, then **Run anyway**, or first check the zip against the
`SHA256SUMS.txt` published with each release.
